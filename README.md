# Cassino Card Game

A simple Cassino card game built with Godot, utilizing a flexible card framework.

## Cassino Overview
Cassino is a card game played with 2-6 players, using a 52-card deck. Players aim to capture cards by pairing or combining them to match the value of a card in their hand.

## How to Play
- Dealer deals 4 cards to each player and 4 to the table.
- Players take turns playing a card to capture table cards, sweep, call, build, or trail.
- Capturing: pair or combine cards to match the played card's value.
- Sweeping: capture all table cards, scoring 1 point.
- Points are scored at the end of each hand, and the player/team with the most points wins.

## Royal Cassino Variant
- Same rules as Cassino, but with court card values:
  - Jacks: 11
  - Queens: 12
  - Kings: 13
  - Aces: 1 or 14 (player's choice)
- Allows for more complex captures, e.g., Queen (12) can capture Ace (1) and Jack (11) or 7 and 5.
